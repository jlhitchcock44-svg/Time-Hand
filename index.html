What it does

  • Priced by your localRates and classifications for 45+ outside line agreements, each marked as taken from the agreement or estimated. Not listed? Enter your own rates.
  • Storm or regular workTap Working, Standby, or Travel as the day changes. It handles double time after long hours, short rest between shifts, Sundays, and holidays.
  • Every benefit fund, separatelyAnnuity, pension, health and welfare, NEBF, plus dues and anything else off your check, for the shift and the whole job.
  • Over scale coveredGetting more than the contract? Add dollars an hour, a percent, your own rate, or your own storm multiplier.
  • Forgot to clock in? Fix itSet the real start time and the clock catches up. Add yesterday or a whole past storm, including days that were part travel and part work.
  • Notes on every shiftNo lunch but dinner furnished, past 16 on a ticket waiting on a switching order. Write it down while you remember it.
  • Show off your checkTurn a shift, a day, or a whole storm into a picture for Facebook or TikTok. You choose whether it shows your local, classification, and benefits. Your name never goes on it.
  • Check your stubCopy a timesheet with hours at each rate, every fund, and your notes, shift by shift.

The fine print

Terms

Time Hand gives estimates using the rates and rules shown for each agreement. It isn't payroll, and it isn't legal or financial advice. Agreements change, so confirm rates with your hall and your agreement. Your subscription renews each year until you cancel.

Privacy

Your shifts, notes and settings stay on your phone. Time Hand has no accounts and doesn't collect, track, or sell your data. When you enter a license key, the app checks it with Lemon Squeezy, which handles checkout and billing under its own privacy policy.

Refunds

If Time Hand doesn't work for you, email within 14 days of paying and you'll get a full refund.
